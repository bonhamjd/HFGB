Quick answers

Questions People Ask

A few short answers about High Flyers Golf Bar in Humphrey, Nebraska.

Can I order pickup online?

Yes. Order online for pickup at 302 Main St. Online ordering is open 11 AM to 9 PM, with pickup from 11:30. On Mondays, ordering starts at 3 PM and pickup starts at 3:30.

What are your hours?

Monday 3 PM to 1 AM, Tuesday through Saturday 11 AM to 1 AM, and Sunday 11 AM to 10 PM.

Where is High Flyers Golf Bar?

302 Main St, Humphrey, NE 68642.

What is your phone number?

(402) 923-0160. You can also email highflyersgolfbar@gmail.com.

Do you show Husker games?

Yes. We show Huskers football, volleyball and basketball, plus the NFL. If there is a game, it is on here.

Do you have a patio?

Yes. The patio out back is always open. We do not have heaters.

Do you take reservations or private parties?

No. We do not take reservations or book private parties. Walk in anytime we are open.

What time does the kitchen close?

The kitchen closes at 9 PM every night. Online ordering stops at 9 PM, with pickup from 11:30. On Mondays, ordering starts at 3 PM and pickup starts at 3:30. The bar stays open until 1 AM Monday through Saturday, and until 10 PM on Sunday.

The best spot in Humphrey to grab a drink, eat good food, and catch the game. Right on Main Street.
